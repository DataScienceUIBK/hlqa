# Tutorial Syllabus

## Title
HLQA: Human-in-the-Loop Question Answering

## Goal
This tutorial introduces Human-in-the-Loop Question Answering as a design perspective for collaborative reasoning-oriented QA systems.

## Tentative Outline

1. From Answering to Cognitive Collaboration
2. Cognitive and Interaction Foundations
3. Adaptive and Socratic Questioning
4. Hint Generation and Guided Reasoning
5. Explanation Generation for Reflection and Understanding
6. Personalized Collaborative QA
7. Applications of HLQA and Human-AI Collaboration
8. Open Challenges and Future Directions

## Expected Audience
Researchers and practitioners in NLP, HCI, educational AI, information retrieval, explainable AI, and human-AI interaction.

## Learning Outcomes
Participants will learn how to:
- understand the cognitive implications of LLM-based QA;
- design QA systems that sustain reasoning rather than only deliver answers;
- use adaptive questioning, hints, and explanations as reasoning-support mechanisms;
- evaluate collaborative QA systems from a human-centered perspective.
