Overview

Human-in-the-Loop Question Answering (HLQA) is a design perspective that places human reasoning, reflection, and cognitive engagement at the center of question answering systems. Rather than treating QA as a one-step answer delivery task, HLQA reframes interaction with AI systems as an adaptive collaborative reasoning process.

Human-Centered QA

Reframing question answering from direct answer delivery into collaborative reasoning support.

Collaborative Reasoning

Supporting users through adaptive interactions that sustain engagement and reflection.

Cognitive Support

Designing AI systems that help users think with AI systems rather than depend on them.

Adaptive Interaction Process

HLQA integrates complementary reasoning-support mechanisms that can be used adaptively depending on user responses, reasoning progress, and interaction context.

STEP 1

Adaptive Questioning

Generate adaptive or Socratic questions that stimulate reasoning based on users’ goals, expertise, and cognitive needs.

STEP 2

Hint Generation

Provide progressively informative hints that scaffold intermediate reasoning without directly revealing final answers.

STEP 3

Explanation Generation

Reinforce understanding and reflection through explanations that consolidate reasoning and long-term cognitive engagement.
