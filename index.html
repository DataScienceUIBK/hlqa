Human-Centered QA Adaptive Questioning Hint Generation Explanation Personalization

Abstract

The tutorial reframes QA from direct answer delivery to collaborative reasoning support. It focuses on three mechanisms: adaptive questioning to stimulate thought, hinting to scaffold intermediate steps, and explanations to reinforce understanding and reflection. The central objective is to reduce cognitive offloading risks and build AI systems that support sustained human reasoning.

Tutorial Structure

1) From Answering to Assisting

Evolution from retrieval/extractive QA to LLM-based reasoning and human-centered design rationale.

2) Cognitive Foundations

Cognitive load, scaffolding, metacognition, and collaborative interaction principles.

3) Adaptive Questioning

Socratic prompts, decomposition, and learner-aware question generation with quality/effectiveness evaluation.

4) Hinting

Progressive guidance strategies that preserve user agency while improving task progression.

5) Explanation

Pedagogical explanation generation for understanding, reflection, and trust calibration.

6) Personalization & Systems

User modeling, knowledge tracing, and deployment in educational and research-assistance settings.
