EMNLP Tutorial Proposal

From Answers to Assistance: Human-in-the-Loop Question Answering in GenAI Era

Jamshid Mozafari, Anubhav Jangra, Smaranda Muresan, Ekaterina Kochmar, Adam Jatowt

University of Innsbruck, Columbia University, MBZUAI

This project page summarizes the tutorial proposal with a focus on adaptive questioning, hinting, and explanation as core mechanisms for human-centered reasoning support.

At a Glance

8Core Parts
4Research Areas
50-100Expected Audience
  • Focus: Human-in-the-Loop Question Answering (HLQA) for collaborative reasoning with AI
  • Scope: Natural Language Processing (NLP), Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), cognitive science, and educational AI
  • Goal: Sustain human reasoning, reflection, and learning, rather than only delivering final answers
