Overview

Human-in-the-Loop Question Answering (HLQA) is a design perspective that places human reasoning, reflection, and cognitive engagement at the center of question answering systems. Rather than treating QA as a one-step answer delivery task, HLQA reframes interaction with AI systems as an adaptive collaborative reasoning process.

Adaptive Questioning

Systems generate adaptive or Socratic questions that encourage users to actively reason about problems.

→

Hint Generation

Systems provide progressively informative guidance that scaffolds intermediate reasoning steps.

→

Explanation Generation

Systems reinforce understanding and reflection rather than merely presenting final answers.
